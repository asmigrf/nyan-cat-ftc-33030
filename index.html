About Us

Nyan Cat #33030 is a FIRST Tech Challenge team. We design, build and program a robot to compete in this season's game, BIOBUZZ, where alliances score Pollen and Nectar into Hives and Flowers.

Our team covers every part of the challenge: mechanical design and CAD, programming, driving, the engineering portfolio, outreach and sponsorship.
